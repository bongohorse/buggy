# Alpine Challenge gameplay update

## Controls and handling

- GAS / D / Right accelerates forward. When reversing, it first brakes, then pulls forward.
- BRAKE / A / Left brakes. Keep holding at near-zero speed for 0.45 seconds to engage a limited reverse gear (about 15km/h). The pedal changes to REV and the dashboard shows R.
- In the air GAS raises the nose and BRAKE lowers it. Short inputs keep both wheels aligned with the landing.
- Motor load ramps in; angular damping and bounded air torque limit uncontrolled spin. A bounded, brief landing compression adds visual weight.
- Dirt, grass, stone and wood change wheel friction and grounded rolling drag. The rolling drag is removed in the air. Dust, grass clippings and gravel colours follow the actual surface.

## 597m course

The familiar opening is followed by a split-level Skyline shortcut, two actual ravine gaps, a tall Summit jump and a rhythm section. Fast, well-aligned approaches reach the raised route; the lower valley remains open. The raised trail uses one-sided collision to prevent an artificial ceiling on the lower path.

The continuous visible road still uses smooth cubic curves sampled every 0.1m. Main and raised paths share their render/collision profiles. Bridge and ravine gaps contain no hidden static road. Warning boards and HUD messages introduce the upcoming decisions. Three optional trail tokens reward more adventurous lines.

## Retry and practice

RETRY / R immediately restarts. After a crash, GAS also restarts; holding that pedal carries into the new attempt. Practice is optional, with six checkpoints (117, 237, 307, 381, 451 and 521m from start). Practice retries restore the last reached checkpoint and reset the bridge. NEW FULL RUN returns to a timed run from the start. Practice finishes never change personal records or medals.

## Small progression scope

One local personal best and three medal levels: Gold <=65s, Silver <=90s, Bronze for finishing. No upgrade shop, additional vehicle or additional biome. Records are versioned for this course. Tuning remains available; these are local open-tuning records, not a competitive leaderboard. Ghost racing is deferred.

## Original sound

Nine original procedural mono effects are generated by `tools/generate_audio.py` at 22.05kHz: a load/speed-dependent engine, four rolling surfaces, landing thump, bridge creak, pickup and checkpoint. SOUND ON/OFF persists locally. Pausing stops the audio loops and effects. Playback uses Defold sound components, not a parallel JavaScript sound implementation.

Sound properties/API: https://defold.com/ref/sound-lua/ and https://defold.com/manuals/sound/.

## Validation

Actual native and WASM Box2D course tests complete the course in about 54.38s, including the raised route; the valley is checked independently. The controlled run has no roof-contact frames. Holding gas alone crashes at about 495.8m. Four real coasting-retention measurements differ (dirt .912, grass .653, stone .785, wood .870 after two seconds). These are deterministic host simulation checks, not a human difficulty or mobile performance claim.

The user accepted the previous visual/mobile state and requested that acceptance work be skipped. This update still runs targeted physics, resource and actual Defold engine/GUI regression checks. No new visual browser or real-device sound/touch acceptance is claimed.
